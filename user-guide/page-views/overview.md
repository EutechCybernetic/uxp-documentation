# Page views

Page views tell you how your account is used: who opened which page, when, and for how long. It is recorded for you automatically, and you read it back on a dashboard.

## What is recorded

For every logged-in user, the platform notes each page they open, the time they open it, and how long they stay before moving on. Visitors who are not logged in are not recorded.

Only which page was opened and by whom is kept. The pages people visit are grouped by their address, so the report reads as a tidy list of pages rather than thousands of near-identical rows.

## Where to see it

Open **Administration > Monitoring > Page Views**. You need the account-management permission to reach it.

The page is a dashboard made up of several widgets:

- **Summary cards** — total page views, unique users, sessions, average time on a page, and who is active right now.
- **Views over time** — a trend line of activity across the range you are looking at.
- **Top pages** and **Most active users** — the busiest pages and the people using them most.
- **Peak periods** and **Views by hour of day** — when in the day activity is highest.
- **Engagement** and **Recent views** — how involved people are, and a live list of the latest visits.

Each widget has a link in its footer that opens a fuller drill-down view. Clicking a page or a user in a list opens a detail panel for just that page or person. What you are looking at is kept in the page's web address, so you can reload it or share the link and land on the same view.

## Filtering

The Page Views page has filters you set once and every widget follows. Narrow the report to a date range, a single page, or a single user, and the whole dashboard — and any drill-down you open from it — updates to match.

## Making it your own

The Page Views page is an ordinary dashboard, so you can rearrange its widgets like any other. If you want the original layout back, use the **Restore** action to return it to the shipped default.

## Turning tracking on or off

Tracking is controlled per account with a switch at **General Settings > General > "Enable page view tracking"**. Changing it takes effect after the page is reloaded.

When tracking is off:

- The Page Views page shows a short notice with a **Go to General Settings** button instead of the dashboard.
- Any Page Views widget placed on another dashboard shows a small notice in place of its content.

Turn the switch back on and reload, and recording and the dashboards resume.

## How long data is kept

By default, page-view data is kept forever. An administrator can set a limit at **General Settings > General > "Page view retention (days)"**. Leave it empty or set it to 0 to keep everything; set a number of days and anything older is removed automatically each day.

## Good to know

- The last page of a session has no recorded duration — a page's time is worked out when the next page is opened, so there is nothing after the final one.
- The Page Views page counts itself, since it is a page like any other.
